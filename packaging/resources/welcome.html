Paperface 50 @VERSION@

An unofficial, non-commercial software emulation of the Serge Paperface 50th Anniversary Edition modular system: the HELIOS voice, PARIS-FLASH slopes and TEMPO DI ROMA sequencing panels, fully banana-patchable. Nothing sounds until you patch it. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Random*Source or Serge Modular.

On the next screen tick what you want: the instrument (MIDI in) and/or the FX version (audio in), each as Audio Unit, VST3 and/or standalone app. Plug-ins go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins; apps to /Applications. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW afterwards; they appear under Black Tie Ventures.

Requires macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
